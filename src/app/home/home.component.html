About Aspenleaf

Shetland Sheepdogs in Dewy Rose, Georgia

Aspenleaf Shelties is a small Shetland Sheepdog breeder in Dewy Rose, Georgia. Since 2008, our program has been rooted in deep respect for the breed, careful planning, and dogs raised as part of the family.

We care deeply about breed type, sound structure, stable temperament, and the kind of Shelties that thrive both in the ring and in everyday life.

Raised With Care

Shelties raised with attention to temperament, structure, and everyday life.

Our puppies are handled thoughtfully from the beginning and grow up as part of the rhythm of our home. We care deeply about confidence, soundness, and the kind of Sheltie that is both beautiful to live with and true to the breed.

That balance matters to us, whether a puppy grows into a cherished companion, a performance dog, or a promising show prospect.
