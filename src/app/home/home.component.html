Shetland Sheepdogs in Dewy Rose, Georgia

Aspenleaf Shelties is a small Shetland Sheepdog breeder in Dewy Rose, Georgia. Since 2008, our program has been rooted in deep respect for the breed, careful planning, and dogs that are raised as part of the family rather than in a high-volume kennel setting.

We compete in AKC conformation and performance events, and we care deeply about the Shetland Sheepdog in both beauty and function. Over the years, we have bred or owned champions, Best of Breed winners, herding group placers, specialty winners, and cherished family companions.

Every litter is planned with intention. We look for sound structure, correct breed type, stable temperament, and pedigrees that support the kind of Shelties we want to carry forward.

Today, we are located in Dewy Rose, Georgia, and we welcome inquiries from families looking for a thoughtfully bred Sheltie as well as fellow enthusiasts who value lineage, health, and breed quality.
