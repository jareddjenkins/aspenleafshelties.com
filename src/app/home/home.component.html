Aspenleaf Shelties | Dewy Rose, Georgia

Small breed kennel. AKC shows. Shelties raised as part of the family.

We compete in AKC conformation and performance events, and our dogs are first and foremost part of our family. Every litter is planned with care for temperament, structure, health, and the kind of Shelties we love living with every day.

Aspenleaf Shelties began in 2008 and has always been rooted in the idea that good breeding starts with deep respect for the breed. Our dogs are not raised in a high-volume program. They are part of our home, our daily life, and the long-term future we are building for our lines.

We compete in AKC conformation and performance events, and we care deeply about preserving the Shetland Sheepdog in both beauty and function. Over the years, we have bred or owned champions, Best of Breed winners, herding group placers, specialty winners, and cherished family companions.

Every litter is planned with intention. We look for sound structure, beautiful breed type, stable temperament, and pedigrees that support the kind of Shelties we want to preserve for the next generation.

Today, we are located in Dewy Rose, Georgia, and we welcome inquiries from families looking for a thoughtfully bred Sheltie as well as fellow enthusiasts who value lineage, health, and breed quality.
