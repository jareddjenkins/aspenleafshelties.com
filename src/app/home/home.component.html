Aspenleaf Shelties

Beautiful, well-loved Shetland Sheepdogs raised in the heart of family life.

A small preservation-focused program centered on temperament, structure, and dogs that thrive both in the ring and at home.

Aspenleaf Shelties began in 2008 on a small farm in northwest Colorado. Over the years, we have bred or owned champions, Best of Breed winners, herding group placers, specialty winners, and above all, deeply loved family companions.

In 2021, we relocated to northeast Georgia, where our dogs continue to grow up with plenty of room to run, play, rest, and be part of everyday family life.

Because home is at the center of our program, our dogs are not raised at a distance from us. They spend their days outdoors, on the couch, underfoot, and woven into the rhythm of the household.
