About Aspenleaf

Thoughtfully raised Shelties in Dewy Rose, Georgia.

Aspenleaf Shelties is a small preservation breeding program rooted in family life, careful planning, and a genuine love for the Shetland Sheepdog.

I dreamed of owning Shelties from a young age, but waited until family life made room for the time and commitment dogs deserve. When our youngest child became involved in dogs through 4-H, we welcomed our first Sheltie and discovered just how life-changing the breed could be.

Our first Sheltie, DJ, may not have been a conformation dog, but he was the start of everything. Through him, we found obedience, rally, conformation, and a community that shaped the direction of Aspenleaf for years to come.

The Shetland Sheepdog is deeply loyal, affectionate, and responsive. At Aspenleaf Shelties, we aim to preserve the melting expression, sound structure, and sweet temperament the breed is known for.

We study pedigrees, evaluate temperaments, and stay current on health testing so each generation represents a thoughtful step forward. Health and temperament are central to every breeding decision we make.

Our adult Shelties are tested for hips, vWD, thyroid, DMS, and eye certification. We are proud members of the Centennial Shetland Sheepdog Club and the American Shetland Sheepdog Association, and we are recognized by the American Kennel Club as Breeders of Merit.

Most of all, we are grateful for the relationships that have grown alongside our dogs. Many of our puppy buyers remain friends for years, and we love seeing their families return to visit.
