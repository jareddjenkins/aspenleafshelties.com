Nutrition matters. At Aspenleaf Shelties, the health and long-term well-being of our dogs comes first, and that includes paying close attention to what goes into their bowls.

After researching a wide range of foods, we found that Life's Abundance offered the quality, freshness, and ingredient standards we were looking for in a food we could confidently recommend.

Formulated by Dr. Jane Bicks, this food was created to support whole-body health with carefully selected ingredients, balanced protein, supportive vitamins and minerals, omega fatty acids, and digestible carbohydrates for steady energy.

Because the food is nutritionally dense, many owners find that it supports healthy coats, good condition, and efficient feeding without the fillers commonly found in lower quality formulas.

If you are preparing for a new puppy or simply looking for a food we trust in our own program, this is a resource worth exploring.
